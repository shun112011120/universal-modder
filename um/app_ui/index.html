Settings

AI model (Ollama)

Any model in your Ollama works. Models with built-in tool calling (qwen3, llama3.1, …) are the most reliable; models without it, like Gemma 3 or your own GGUF imports, get the tools as text instructions automatically.

Bigger context lets the AI keep more of the guides and files in mind, but uses more GPU memory. On an 8 GB card, 8k is a good start; past what fits, Ollama spills to the CPU and gets 2–3× slower.
